## 🔍 3. Hardware Troubleshooting Case Study

During initial system reassembly and power verification, our team encountered a boot failure (No POST / Display Output Failure):

1. **Root Cause Isolation**: During component disassembly, a team member accidentally touched the gold contact pins (gold fingers) of the RAM module with bare hands. Fingerprint oils and moisture created a micro-insulation layer, preventing proper electrical contact within the DIMM slot.
2. **Resolution & Verification**: We isolated the faulty RAM module, replaced it with a clean module, and re-seated it securely until the DIMM latches clicked. The system successfully booted and restored display output.

## 💡 4. Personal Reflection & Key Takeaways

> *"Hardware reliability starts at the physical layer. Minor contact issues can prevent complex computing systems from performing POST operations."*

As a **Year 1 Computer Network & Security student at UTM**, this hands-on lab provided practical insights that extend beyond textbook theory:
- **Importance of Handling Standards**: I learned the critical importance of handling sensitive electronic components strictly by their plastic edges to prevent Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) damage and pin contamination from skin oils.
- **Physical Layer Diagnostics**: Experiencing real-world troubleshooting first-hand taught me to systematically isolate hardware failures before jumping to software conclusions.
- **Team Collaboration**: Working in a group of 6 under the Lab Assistant's guidance reinforced the value of clear communication and peer-checking during physical assembly processes.
